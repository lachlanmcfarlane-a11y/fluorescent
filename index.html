Your objective is to carve valid words out of the daily 14-segment base word. Execute up to 5 banks to maximize your yield.

MECHANIC 1: SPLICING

Click individual line segments in the display to turn them ON or OFF. Every segment you change consumes 1 splice from your daily budget of 10.

MECHANIC 2: VOIDING & SPACING

Need a gap to separate words? Click the dot at the bottom right of any letter to completely void and dim it. This creates a space between words and does not cost any splices.
